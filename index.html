About This Game

It details the story of Owen Stone, old detective on a case where 5 children have gone missing in an arcade. He has to navigate his way through the strange arcade with only one of the employees helping guide him through and giving out information. Eventually, his search leads him to the basement, where he must play through one of the VR games stashed away and defeat the creature that’s taken hold of it. This is a game that was created with a team for the final project of our college degrees it is both an overtop solve the mystery and also a 2d platformer created 2024

Words On The Page

I order everything I need.
I never leave my apartment—technology takes care of everything for me.

My neighbors seem to live the same way. Their doors stay closed, their deliveries appear on schedule, and I’ve never seen a single one of them step into the hallway.

Yesterday, when I grabbed my burger, I noticed the same order sitting outside my neighbor’s door. Same bag. Same timestamp. Same everything. I figured it was a coincidence.

Today, I ordered pizza—something different, just to break the routine.

When I opened my door, my pizza was waiting for me.
And outside my neighbor’s door… the burger was still there.

Not a new one.
The same one.
Cold now. Grease soaking through the paper. Exactly where it was yesterday.
